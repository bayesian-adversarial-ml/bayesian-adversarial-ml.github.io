Chapter 05

Adversarial issues in unsupervised learning

Attacks and defenses for models that learn without labels. Bayesian methods account for uncertainty in adversarial transformations, protect predictive distributions, and connect to emerging risks in generative models and language-model applications.

In this chapter

  • Generative models
