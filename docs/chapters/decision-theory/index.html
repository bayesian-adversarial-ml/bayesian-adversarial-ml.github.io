Chapter 07

A Bayesian decision theoretic perspective on adversarial machine learning

Robustness is assessed through the quality of the decisions a model supports. Utility gaps connect adversarial risk analysis, minimax protection and least-favorable attack distributions, leading to a pipeline for computing, monitoring and revising defenses.
